Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 October 2026

Hasahya Motivated ("we", "us") makes Christian devotional videos, study ebooks and practical services, and runs software that helps publish the videos we make. This policy explains what personal information we handle and why.

Who we are

Hasahya Motivated, Uganda. Contact: brightbrio@gmail.com or WhatsApp +256 753 517508.

What we collect

  • Orders: the name and phone number you give us when you order an ebook or service, and what you ordered.
  • Payments: payments are made through mobile money providers. We receive confirmation of the payment and the amount. We do not store your mobile money PIN or card details.
  • Messages: anything you send us on WhatsApp or email, so we can answer you.
  • Publishing accounts: where our software is connected to a YouTube, Facebook or TikTok account, it uses the access that account's owner grants. It uses that access only to publish the videos we make to that account. We do not read private messages or personal data from those platforms.

How we use it

  • To deliver the ebook or service you ordered, and to send receipts.
  • To answer your questions.
  • To publish our own videos to our own channels.

What we do not do

We do not sell personal information. We do not share it with advertisers. We do not use it for anything other than the purposes above.

Sharing

We share only what is needed to do the job: the mobile money provider to take payment, and the platform a video is published to. We may also share information if the law requires it.

Keeping it safe

Account access tokens are stored on our own computer and our own server, and are not published. We keep order records only as long as needed for accounts and tax purposes.

Your choices

You can ask us to see, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you. You can also disconnect any publishing account at any time from that platform's own settings, which removes our access.

Children

Our videos are suitable for families. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe we have, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page.
