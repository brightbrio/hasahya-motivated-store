Hasahya Motivated

A Christian devotional & motivation media channel — and the software behind it.

Hasahya Motivated makes short devotional videos, Bible stories and encouragement content, published daily to YouTube, Facebook and TikTok. This page describes the publishing software itself, for anyone reviewing its platform-API access.

What this app is

A small, self-operated publishing tool, built and run by the same person who makes the videos. It takes a finished video we have already produced — narration, captions, our own channel branding — and uploads it to our own connected accounts on YouTube, Facebook and TikTok. It is not a public product, a third-party scheduler for other creators, or anything that handles anyone else's content.

Why it needs each TikTok permission

video.upload / video.publish

To upload the devotional videos this channel produces to the connected TikTok account's own inbox. Nothing is posted without the account holder's own final tap to publish inside the TikTok app.

user.info.basic

Only to confirm which TikTok account the upload is going to (so a video is never sent to the wrong account) — not to read followers, messages, or any other personal data.
